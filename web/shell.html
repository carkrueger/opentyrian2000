Playing with a controller? Connect it and press any button on it now, before you start. Browsers only let a page use a controller after one of its buttons has been pressed, and the game looks for controllers when it starts.

Arrow keys to move, Space to fire, Enter to change fire mode, Ctrl/Alt for sidekicks, Esc for the menu. The mouse works too. Progress is saved in this browser.

OpenTyrian2000 is free software under the GNU GPL (source code). Tyrian 2000 has been released as freeware.

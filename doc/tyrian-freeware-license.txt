Tyrian Freeware License
=======================

Tyrian was developed by Eclipse Software and was published by Epic MegaGames in
1995.  In 2004, Jason Emery released the game as freeware under the following
terms:

    Feel free to play it all you want and share it with friends.

Tyrian 2000, a re-release of Tyrian published by XSIV Games in 1999, has
likewise been released as freeware.  Its data files are distributed at
<https://www.camanis.net/tyrian/tyrian2000.zip>.
